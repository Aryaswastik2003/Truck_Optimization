❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I achieve 100% utilization?
100% utilization is mathematically impossible in real-world 3D bin packing due to:
  • Irregular box sizes: Gaps are inevitable when boxes don't divide evenly
  • Weight distribution: Heavier items must be placed lower
Industry research shows 80-97% is optimal for mixed cargo loads.
Is 80-90% utilization good?
Yes! 80-90% is excellent. This places you in the top 10% of logistics operations. Most professional logistics companies achieve 80-90% utilization. Anything above 95% indicates near-perfect optimization considering real-world constraints.
What does "Weight Limited" vs "Volume Limited" mean?
Weight Limited: The truck reached its maximum weight capacity before filling all available space. Solution: Use lighter boxes or a truck with higher payload capacity.

Volume Limited: The truck ran out of physical space before reaching weight limits. This is normal for bulky, lightweight cargo.

How does this compare to manual loading?
Manual loading typically achieves 60-75% utilization due to human estimation errors and time constraints. This system uses advanced algorithms to achieve 85-97% utilization, potentially saving 15-30% on transportation costs by reducing the number of required trucks.
